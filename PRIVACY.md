# Privacy

Froxel collects no personal data and sends no telemetry. It has no accounts,
no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Phones and tablets

The iOS and Android apps make no network connections. Your models, renders,
and the app's log stay in the app's own documents folder on the device. They
leave it only when you share or copy a file yourself, for example through the
Files app on iOS.

## Desktop

The desktop app opens network connections only when you ask for them: the
`--control` and `--bridge` ports listen on `127.0.0.1`, and
`froxel mcp --connect` connects to the address you pass it. Nothing is sent
anywhere else.
