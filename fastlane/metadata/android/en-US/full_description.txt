Froxel is a voxel editor for Android phones and tablets. Build models cube by cube, paint them from the palette, and render them with a real path tracer that runs on the GPU.

EDIT WITH TOUCH
• One finger uses the active tool. Two fingers orbit and pinch to zoom. Three fingers pan.
• Brush, line, circle, square, box, and face fill.
• Generators and mutators.
• Marquee, box select, and flood select.
• Add, remove, and paint modes, with undo for every stroke.
• Long-press the colour, then tap a voxel, to pick its colour.

RENDER
• Render mode path-traces the scene with sky, sun, metal, glass, and emission.
• The image keeps refining while you look at it. Save Render writes a PNG.

MAGICAVOXEL FILES
• Opens and saves .vox files, keeping groups, layers, hidden objects, and materials.
• Files saved in Froxel open in MagicaVoxel and on the desktop version of Froxel.
• Your models stay in the app's storage.

TABLETS
• On a tablet the tools and the palette sit beside the viewport, as on the desktop.

Try it on the bundled frog and lily pad, under the folder button.

Froxel is open source (MIT) and also runs on macOS, Windows, Linux, FreeBSD, and iOS. It collects no data, needs no account, and asks for no permissions.
